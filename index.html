OUR STORY

Right where students actually want to be

Perched near Nicolaus Copernicus University's Gagarina campus, Coffee To Go has become the unofficial second office for students and staff alike. Calm atmosphere, cool lounge music, proper desks for working, and reliable Wi-Fi — alongside genuinely good coffee, beer, and a fresh orange juice regulars specifically call out. Warm, relaxed, and exactly where you'd want to be between lectures.
